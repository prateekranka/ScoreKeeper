# ScoreKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

ScoreKeeper is designed to keep game-night information on your device.

## Data stored on your device

ScoreKeeper stores player names, game settings, scores, rounds, game history, saved rosters, appearance preferences, the introductory game-count state, and review-prompt eligibility locally using SwiftData and UserDefaults. This information is used only to provide scoring, history, statistics, rematches, purchase access, and preference features.

ScoreKeeper does not transmit this information to the developer and does not provide cloud sync.

## Purchases

ScoreKeeper uses Apple StoreKit for its optional one-time Unlimited Games purchase. Payment information is entered and processed by Apple outside ScoreKeeper. The developer does not receive payment-card or bank information. ScoreKeeper checks Apple-provided transaction entitlement information to determine whether the purchase is unlocked.

## Data collection and tracking

ScoreKeeper has no advertising, analytics SDK, third-party tracking, developer-operated server, account system, or online sharing feature. The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal data through the app.

## Retention and deletion

Game data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Removing the app removes its local game data. Apple may retain the purchase record associated with your Apple Account so a non-consumable purchase can be restored; Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

## Children’s privacy

ScoreKeeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. It does not provide accounts, messaging, public profiles, or online content.

## Changes

If ScoreKeeper’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is distributed.
