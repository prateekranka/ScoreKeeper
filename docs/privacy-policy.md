# ScoreKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

ScoreKeeper is designed to keep game-night information private and on your device.

## Data ScoreKeeper Stores

ScoreKeeper stores player names, game settings, scores, rounds, game history, saved rosters, appearance preferences, free-game allowance state, and review-prompt eligibility locally on your device. The free-game count is also stored in this device’s private Keychain so reinstalling the app does not restart the 25-game allowance. This information is used only to provide the app’s scorekeeping, history, statistics, rematch, purchase-access, and preference features.

ScoreKeeper does not transmit this information to the developer and does not provide cloud sync.

## Purchases

ScoreKeeper uses Apple StoreKit for its optional one-time Unlimited Games purchase. Payment information is entered and processed by Apple outside ScoreKeeper. The developer does not receive payment-card or bank information. ScoreKeeper checks Apple-provided transaction entitlement information to determine whether Unlimited Games is unlocked.

## Data Collection and Tracking

ScoreKeeper does not include advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, or developer-operated servers. The developer does not collect personal data through the app and does not sell or share app data with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Game data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Deleting the app removes its local game data. The anonymous free-game count may remain in the device Keychain so the introductory allowance cannot be reset by reinstalling; it contains no player names, scores, account details, or other game data. Apple may retain the purchase record associated with your Apple Account so that a non-consumable purchase can be restored; Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

## Children’s Privacy

ScoreKeeper does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. It does not provide accounts, messaging, public profiles, or online sharing.

## Changes

If ScoreKeeper’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is distributed.
