# PipCount Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

PipCount is designed to keep game-night information on your device.

## Data stored on your device

PipCount stores player names, game settings, scores, rounds, game history, saved rosters, appearance preferences, and review-prompt eligibility locally using SwiftData and UserDefaults. The free-game allowance is stored separately in a device-only Keychain item. When migrating from the legacy UserDefaults game count, PipCount keeps the maximum of the Keychain count and the legacy count so an update cannot reduce the allowance history. That Keychain allowance survives app deletion and reinstall on the same device, but it does not move to another device and is not synced or uploaded. These values are used only to provide scoring, history, statistics, rematches, purchase access, and preference features.

PipCount does not transmit this information to the developer and does not provide cloud sync.

## Handwriting and score entry

Scoreboard scoring can read a handwritten number with Apple’s on-device Vision tools. The ink image is processed on the device to suggest a score from 0 to 99. PipCount asks you to confirm that number, redraw, or type the score before it is saved. Temporary ink used for recognition is not uploaded and is not stored as a photo library or cloud file. You can also type a score from 0 to 99 without drawing.

## User-directed sharing

After a game, you can share a scorecard image through the system share sheet. PipCount creates that image on the device. It does not upload the image or player data to a PipCount server. If you choose another app, AirDrop, or a message, that destination’s own privacy rules apply. Sharing is optional and happens only when you tap Share.

## Purchases

PipCount uses Apple StoreKit for its optional one-time Unlimited Games purchase. Payment information is entered and processed by Apple outside PipCount. The developer does not receive payment-card or bank information. PipCount checks Apple-provided transaction entitlement information to determine whether the purchase is unlocked.

## Data collection and tracking

PipCount has no advertising, analytics SDK, third-party tracking, developer-operated server, or account system. The developer does not collect, sell, or share personal data through the app. PipCount does not operate its own online sharing service. Any share you start uses Apple’s system share sheet at your direction.

## Retention and deletion

Game data, saved rosters, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Removing the app removes that local SwiftData and UserDefaults data. The free-game allowance intentionally remains in the device-only Keychain across deletion and reinstall on the same device; it is not transferred to another device. Apple may retain the purchase record associated with your Apple Account so a non-consumable purchase can be restored; Apple’s handling of that information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

## Children’s privacy

PipCount does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. It does not provide accounts, messaging, public profiles, or online content.

## Changes

If PipCount’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the affected version is distributed.
