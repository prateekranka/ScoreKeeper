# PipCount growth ideas

## Hard paywall

Built in this branch:

1. **Onboarding, then paywall.** No free games. Yearly plan with 3 days free, preselected. Lifetime as the second option.
2. **Exit offer.** First time someone taps X, a second paywall offers 50% off the yearly plan. Shown once per person.
3. **Gift on the app icon.** Long-press the icon (the same menu that has Remove App) shows "50% off gift". Only for people who closed the paywall. Gone once they pay.
4. **Notifications only until they pay.** Trial-ends-tomorrow reminder, one gift reminder, and up to 4 Friday "Game night?" nudges. All removed the moment someone pays.
5. **Old users keep their promise.** Anyone who already used the app keeps the free games they were promised.

Worth testing next:

6. **Paywall at the "Start game" tap instead of after onboarding.** People pick a game and add their friends first. They feel invested before they see a price.
7. **"Remind me before my trial ends" switch on the paywall.** It raises trial starts because it removes the fear of a surprise charge.
8. **Dismiss survey instead of a straight discount.** One question: why not now? Too expensive → 50% off. Not sure → 3-line feature recap.
9. **Host pays, friends play.** Turn on Family Sharing for the subscription and say so on the paywall. Game nights are group events.
10. **Lifetime as the price anchor.** A high lifetime price next to the yearly price makes the yearly feel small.
11. **App Store win-back offers.** Apple shows these to people whose subscription ended, also inside the App Store. Run one each holiday season.
12. **Skip a weekly plan.** It earns fast but brings 1-star reviews for a casual app.

## Mascot

**Recommendation: "Pip", a black dot with tiny legs.**

- It is the big black circle already in the app's Bauhaus art. It is a dice pip. It is the app's name.
- Red square, blue square, yellow square are its friends (the other shapes in the art). Each friend can be a player.
- A simple shape stays the same in every AI image and is easy to animate in Rive.
- The gold trophy (ScoreCup, already drawn) is a prop. It shows up only when someone wins.

## Social (TikTok and Instagram)

Videos:

- **"The friend who always forgets the score."** Short skits. Pip is the friend who never forgets.
- **POV game night fights.** "POV: someone says they had 40 points last round." Cut to PipCount's round log and undo.
- **Comic-strip videos.** 4 to 6 AI comic panels of Pip at game night, with voice-over. Comic art is easy to remember and people trust it more than stock photos.
- **iPhone Duo table mode.** Unfold the Duo, stand it on the table, everyone sees the scoreboard. Few apps show off the Duo yet, so this is fresh.

Carousels:

- "5 card games for 6 or more people" with how to score each. Last slide: PipCount does the math.
- "How to score Phase 10 without a fight."
- "Your friend group's stats, wrapped." Built from the share card.
- Pip comic carousels: one joke per slide, app on the last slide.

## App Store and other channels

- **Autoplay preview video.** In iOS 27, App Store banners can autoplay video. Preview videos can lift installs by up to 30%. Make a 15-second Pip video: unfold Duo, score a round, Pip celebrates.
- **Every share is an ad.** Put a small Pip and the app name on the shared scorecard image.
- **Get on Paul Hudson's iPhone Duo list.** He is posting apps that are "great on iPhone Duo". Send him a short clip after this release.
- **Holiday timing.** Thanksgiving and Christmas are the biggest game-night weeks. Post carousels from early November. Run the win-back offer in December.
- **Apple Search Ads.** Keywords: "phase 10 score", "score keeper", "card game score", "game night".
