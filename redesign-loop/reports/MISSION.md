# Mission

ScoreKeeper is a native-feeling iPhone scorekeeping app for casual game nights:
choose a game mode, add players, score rounds with big tactile steppers, use a
timer tool, and turn finished games into history, stats, and rematches. It must
feel playful, capable, and polished — precise enough to trust with the final
score, fast enough that the phone never becomes the center of attention. The
visual language is "Paper Bauhaus": warm printed-paper surfaces, hard-edged
geometric ink shapes, one ultramarine accent, yellow reserved for the winner.
This prototype must look and move like an AAA shipping app — elite typography,
disciplined color, and expertly crafted motion on every interaction.
