# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People hosting or joining casual game nights who need a fast, low-friction way to set up players, keep score, use table tools, and remember past results without interrupting the game.

## Product Purpose

ScoreKeeper is a native iOS scorekeeping app for game nights. It helps users choose a game mode, add a crew, score rounds, use live play tools, and turn finished games into history, stats, and rematches. Success means the phone supports the table without becoming the center of attention.

## Brand Personality

Playful, capable, polished. The product should feel friendly enough for a living-room game night and precise enough to trust with the final score.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS onboarding, heavy marketing copy, permission-driven first launch flows, paywall-first experiences, mascot art that feels unrelated to scorekeeping, and visual treatments that make the app feel slower than paper.

## Design Principles

- Keep the table moving: every screen should reduce interruptions and make the next action obvious.
- Show the game loop: onboarding and empty states should demonstrate setup, scoring, tools, and history through concrete app-like moments.
- Make trust visible: score totals, history, rematches, and undo affordances should feel dependable and easy to verify.
- Stay playful without losing control: color and motion can carry energy, but controls must remain familiar, readable, and reachable.
- Respect first launch: explain value quickly, allow skipping, and avoid monetization or review prompts before the user has played.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native iOS controls and semantic typography where practical, maintain 44pt minimum touch targets, support Dynamic Type and Dark Mode, avoid relying on color alone for meaning, and keep motion short with reduced-motion alternatives.
