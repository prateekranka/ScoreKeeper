Club Table

A tactile, restrained system for a shared phone on a crowded game-night table. The app should feel present and social, but the score sheet stays quiet enough to read at a glance.

Positioning: the friendly table captain that keeps the group moving without becoming the center of attention.

Physical scene
Dim living room, phone beside cards and snacks
Target surface
iPhone 16 Pro on iOS 26+, 402 x 874 CSS px
Navigation model
Home table, sheet setup, sticky scoring action
Motion posture
150-200 ms pressed states and numeric score flips

Tokens

Deep green environmental shell, clear light sheets, copper warmth, and status colors tuned for readable contrast.

Components

  • iOS 26-style glass treatment is reserved for bars and tappable controls.
  • Table mats for active games and current rounds.
  • Player placards with initials, totals, and compact steppers.

States

  • Roster warnings stay inline beside the affected setup flow.
  • Leading players use total color and a text badge, not decoration.
  • Disabled actions remain visible with clear requirements.

Representative Screens

Same product flow across every direction, framed as iPhone 16 Pro screens: home, setup, generic scoring, Phase 10, and game summary/history detail.
