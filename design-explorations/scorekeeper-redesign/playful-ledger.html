Playful Ledger

A colorful score ledger for families and friends, where player identity colors make scanning easier and controls are large enough to pass the phone around without precision tapping.

Positioning: bright, forgiving, and state-rich without turning the score sheet into a toy.

Physical scene
Shared phone moving around a couch or kitchen table
Target surface
iPhone 16 Pro on iOS 26+, 402 x 874 CSS px
Navigation model
Activity home, friendly setup steps, player-led score rows
Motion posture
Quick confirmations, button compression, reduced-motion fallback

Tokens

Light neutral base with coral action, blue totals, green success, and violet player/system accents.

Components

  • iOS 26-style glass treatment is reserved for bars and tappable controls.
  • Ledger strips combine identity, total, current round, and quick actions.
  • Setup uses visible chips for saved players and error recovery.

States

  • Player colors are always paired with names, never color alone.
  • Warnings and empty states explain the next action inline.
  • Score controls keep stable dimensions so totals never shift layout.

Representative Screens

This direction keeps the same iPhone 16 Pro workflows but makes every player, round, and status easier to spot from arm's length.
